About Us

Sklénikum was founded in 2008 by a family of passionate gardeners in the heart of Liberec. What started as a small workshop crafting custom garden structures has grown into one of the Czech Republic's leading greenhouse retailers.

We believe every garden deserves a greenhouse — whether you tend a balcony herb garden or a sprawling market plot. That's why we offer everything from compact cold frames to full-sized glass structures, alongside our unique configure-your-own service.

All our products are tested in our own demonstration garden and come with a minimum 5-year structural warranty.
